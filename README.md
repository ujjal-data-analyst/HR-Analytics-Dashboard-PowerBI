## Project Overview

This HR Analytics Dashboard was built to analyze employee attrition, workforce demographics, salary distribution, and department-wise employee trends.

The dashboard helps HR teams and business managers identify key factors affecting employee retention and organizational performance.

---



## Key Metrics

- Total Employees
- Active Employees
- Attrition Count
- Attrition Rate
- Average Employee Age
- Average Experience

---

## Key Insights

### Employee Attrition Analysis
- Highest attrition observed in the Administration and Sales departments.
- Employees with lower salary slabs showed comparatively higher attrition.

### Workforce Demographics
- Majority employees belong to the 26–35 age group.
- Male employees represent a larger percentage of attrition.

### Experience Trend
- Employees with lower experience levels showed higher attrition frequency.
